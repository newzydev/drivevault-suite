DriveVault Browser Pro - User Guide (version 0.7.0, beta)

A web browser for Windows built on Chromium (Qt WebEngine) with an ad blocker, picture in picture and a video saver. Everything stays on this computer: there is no account, no sync and no usage data is sent to DriveVault.

Browsing

Tabs (new, close, duplicate, pin, reopen closed), the address bar (searches Google by default when you type search words), bookmarks (import / export as HTML), history, downloads, find in page (Ctrl+F), zoom per site, full screen, save as PDF, view source, developer tools, and a private window that keeps nothing on the disk. Sites that ask for the camera, microphone, location and similar ask you first; notifications are refused by default.

Use the Thai / EN switch in the title bar to change the app language. Click the gear or the three-dot menu to open a centered dialog. The Home button opens Google by default. Set a custom Home page in Settings to change it.

The main window reopens at its previous size and position or maximized. Double-click the title bar to maximize or restore it. Press F11 to toggle browser full screen.

The new tab workspace has a bundled wallpaper with two tabs: Shortcuts shows Google and your bookmarks, and Most visited shows sites from your history. Right-click the workspace or an ordinary web page background to open the dark page menu.

The shield (ad blocker)

The shield button shows how many requests were stopped on the page. The program ships four lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Fanboy's Social Blocking List, Fanboy's Annoyance List) as a snapshot with the date shown in the shield panel. Update filter lists downloads newer copies from easylist.to - only when you press it. You can switch each list, the page-element hiding and the protection scripts on or off, and pause the shield for one site when a site does not work.

Defend against pages that detect ad blockers (on by default) hides "turn off your ad blocker" walls and answers the usual detector scripts. It has been tested on lab pages; it is not a guarantee: a site that checks on its own server, or that hides its content until an advertisement was shown, can still refuse to work.

Picture in picture

The picture-in-picture button (or Alt+P) moves the tab into a small window that stays on top; the video fills it and keeps playing. Videos in the main page only.

Saving videos

A button with a count appears when the page loaded a video or audio file or an HLS / DASH playlist. A file is saved as it is; an HLS / DASH stream is joined into one .mp4 without re-encoding by FFmpeg (installed with the program). The progress is in the downloads panel.

Save only what you have the right to keep. Streams that are copy protected (DRM) are refused, and YouTube and subscription or music streaming services are not offered, because their terms forbid it.

Limits of this version

  • No extensions (Qt 6.8 cannot load them) and no Google sign-in or sync.
  • The engine has no H.264 / AAC codecs, so some videos do not play.
  • The program is not made the default browser; do that in Windows Settings if you want.
  • The installer is not code-signed.

Your data

History, bookmarks, settings, cookies and updated filter lists are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DriveVault\BrowserPro. Uninstalling the program does not delete them or your saved videos.
