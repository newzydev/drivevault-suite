# drivevault-video-editor-pro/resources - rules (adds to the parent files)

- `translations/drivevault_th_TH.ts` is the Thai translation (source language English). It is generated by `update_translations` and then filled in; the build must have **0 unfinished** entries. Never hand-edit source strings in the `.ts`.
- `fonts/`: Inter and Noto Sans Thai (SIL OFL 1.1) with their license texts - keep the `OFL-*.txt` files, they are shipped in `licenses\`. The same fonts are also in `apps/drivevault-master-pro/resources/fonts`.
- `icons/`: `app.ico` / `app.png` (product icon, violet), UI icons are drawn from `src/ui/components/IconData.js`.
- Do not add large binaries (video, audio, models) here: tests generate their media, models are downloaded by the user.
